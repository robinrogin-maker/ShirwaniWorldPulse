04 — Clear Space & Spacing

min. height min. height min. width min. width

Maintain a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the capital "S" in "Shirwani" on all sides.
Never place text, images, or graphic elements within this protected area.

05 — Correct Usage

✓ Do

Use the logo on dark backgrounds for maximum impact.

✓ Do

Maintain the logo's proportions when scaling.

✓ Do

Use the simplified icon for favicons, app icons, and small sizes.

✗ Don't

Stretch, distort, or rotate the logo.

✗ Don't

Change the logo colors or apply gradients not in the brand palette.

✗ Don't

Place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds.
